Symbol spin, pilot

The colleague's circle-symbol spin, applied to three marks. Every part of a mark is a flat piece in 3D: it rotates with the whole, projects through a perspective camera (nearer parts grow, farther ones shrink, tilted ones squash) and parts re-stack by depth. Each run starts and ends on the resting mark. Hover a tile to play it, or use Play all.

Preset
Perspective 90° Duration 2.2s

How it maps onto the site. This runs on the same SVG the tiles already draw, with no WebGL and no new files: one small per-frame routine sets a 2D matrix on each part. On the site it would replace the hover motion for these three marks (after the 2s rest), and could also drive the card's Play.
