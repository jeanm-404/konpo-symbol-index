Solid marks, pilot

Seven marks with very different shapes, each turned into a solid. Parts that are true discs (one closed round outline, no hole) can become spheres, and a disc with a flat cut (Dune) becomes a sliced sphere; everything else is extruded with thickness. Spheres only read as 3D through light, so the light comes up as the spin starts and fades as it ends: every mark still starts and ends as the flat white drawing. Hover a tile to play it.
